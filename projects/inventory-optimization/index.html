Inventory Optimization

Replacing static replenishment rules with value- and demand-based inventory policies supported by real-time visibility.

ABC ClassificationSafety StockTableauInventory Planning

The challenge

Static min/max and reorder settings did not reflect differences in item value, demand variability, and lead time. This increased the risk of both excess inventory and material shortages.

My approach

  • Segmented inventory using ABC classification based on value and consumption.
  • Set differentiated min/max, safety-stock, and reorder policies.
  • Aligned replenishment settings with demand and lead-time patterns.
  • Built a Tableau view to monitor stock position and procurement alignment.

Tools

TableauExcelABC AnalysisSafety StockDemand Analysis

The impact

The redesigned policy improved inventory discipline and made excess and shortage risks easier to identify.

$30K

Annual carrying-cost savings from better replenishment policies and reduced excess and obsolete inventory.
