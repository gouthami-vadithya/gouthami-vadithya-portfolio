Centralized Receiving Workflow

Designing a consistent receiving process across three locations—from the SAP Ariba purchase order through inspection, internal delivery, three-way match, and exception escalation.

3 LocationsSAP AribaProcess MappingThree-Way Match

The challenge

Receiving activities varied by location and team. Without a shared workflow, packages, receipts, damage reporting, and invoice matching could be handled inconsistently, creating delays and weak ownership.

My approach

  • Mapped the current state beginning with approved SAP Ariba purchase orders.
  • Defined checkpoints for delivery identification, barcode capture, quantity and damage inspection.
  • Established internal distribution, recipient confirmation, and goods-receipt ownership.
  • Connected receiving outcomes to three-way matching and invoice-exception escalation.
  • Documented roles, handoffs, controls, and KPIs for future SOP and UAT development.

The future-state design created one scalable receiving model with clearer ownership and traceability.
