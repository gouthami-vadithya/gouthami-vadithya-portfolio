Strategic Sourcing & Supplier Negotiation

Using competitive sourcing, financial analysis, and structured negotiation to evaluate the true cost of supplier proposals and deliver measurable savings.

10+ RFx EventsNPV AnalysisBAFO Negotiation$20M+ Spend

The challenge

Supplier quotes could not be compared on price alone. Payment timing, financing costs, scope differences, commercial terms, and long-term obligations all affected the actual value of each proposal.

My approach

  • Translated stakeholder requirements into clear RFQ and RFP packages.
  • Researched suppliers and created bid-leveling models for pricing, scope, lead time, risk, and terms.
  • Built NPV comparisons that incorporated payment timing and reverse-factoring costs.
  • Prepared cost-benefit analyses and used the findings to structure BAFO negotiations.
  • Documented the commercial recommendation and supported supplier selection and contract award.

Tools

The impact

The sourcing approach created a defensible supplier decision based on total evaluated cost—not just the lowest initial quote.

$540K
