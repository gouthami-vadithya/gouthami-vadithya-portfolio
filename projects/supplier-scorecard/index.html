Supplier Performance Scorecard

Turning supplier data into a focused weekly operating rhythm for corrective action, expediting decisions, and performance improvement.

OTD & OTIFQualityCostCorrective Action

The challenge

Supplier performance discussions were reactive and scattered across delivery updates, expedite requests, quality issues, and cost conversations. Teams needed one shared view of performance and risk.

My approach

  • Defined KPIs for delivery, quality, cost, lead time, OTIF, reschedules, expedite rate, and response time.
  • Created a weekly scorecard across concurrent capital programs.
  • Used trends to prioritize suppliers and assign specific corrective actions.
  • Structured supplier reviews around evidence, ownership, and due dates.

Tools

ExcelPower BISAP DataSupplier ReviewsCorrective Action Tracking

The impact

The scorecard gave procurement and stakeholders a common fact base for supplier conversations.

98%

Delivery performance achieved while reducing expedite orders and improving corrective-action accountability.
