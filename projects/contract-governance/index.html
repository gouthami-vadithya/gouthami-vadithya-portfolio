Contract Lifecycle & Renewal Governance

Building a more proactive contract-review process so supplier agreements could be renewed, amended, or escalated before deadlines became operational risks.

30+ AgreementsAgiloft CLMSLA GovernanceRenewal Planning

The challenge

Supplier pricing, amendments, renewal dates, and SLA obligations were managed across agreements with different owners and timelines. Late reviews could create rushed negotiations, expired terms, service risk, and unnecessary escalations.

My approach

  • Reviewed active agreements and organized key dates, pricing terms, amendments, and renewal requirements.
  • Introduced earlier review checkpoints to give business owners, Procurement, Legal, and suppliers sufficient time to act.
  • Created escalation triggers for approaching expirations, incomplete documentation, and unresolved commercial terms.
  • Tracked SLA obligations and renewal status in Agiloft CLM.
  • Coordinated stakeholders through review, negotiation, amendment, and execution.

Tools

Agiloft CLMContract ReviewRenewal TrackingSLA ManagementStakeholder CoordinationSupplier Negotiation

The impact

The governance process shifted contract management from deadline-driven reaction to planned, accountable review.

20%

Faster renewal turnaround across a portfolio of 30+ supplier agreements through earlier review and escalation checkpoints.
