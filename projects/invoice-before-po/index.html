Invoice Before PO Dashboard

Creating one executive view of purchasing exceptions so leaders could see where compliance was breaking, why it was happening, and where action was needed.

Power BISAP AribaExecutive ReportingProcurement Compliance

The challenge

Invoice-before-PO activity was reviewed through fragmented records, making it difficult to identify confirming orders, expired or missing contracts, invoice-date mismatches, and repeat exceptions by supplier or cost center.

My approach

  • Defined exception rules and translated them into clear compliance categories.
  • Combined supplier, PO, invoice, contract, and cost-center data into a reporting model.
  • Designed KPI cards and drill-down visuals for executive and departmental review.
  • Created reason categories so stakeholders could move from identifying an exception to correcting its root cause.

Tools

Power BISAP AribaExcelData ModelingStakeholder Workshops

The impact

The dashboard replaced a fragmented review process with a consistent, decision-ready view of noncompliant spend.

$20M+

Procurement activity supported with clearer exception visibility by supplier, cost center, and compliance reason.
