About Me

Who Am I?

Hi, I’m Gouthami. I started my career close to the everyday realities of materials, suppliers, and production. That experience still shapes how I work.

To me, procurement is not just about issuing a purchase order. I want to understand what the business needs, what is driving the cost, where the risk sits, and what will make the process easier for the people using it.

My work covers sourcing, supplier performance, contracts, receiving, invoice issues, and procurement analytics. I am at my best when a process is unclear or the data does not tell the full story. I ask the right questions, bring the right people together, and turn the problem into something practical.
