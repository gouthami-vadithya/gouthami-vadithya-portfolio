About Me

A little about me.

Hi, I’m Gouthami. I started my career working close to production, where a missing component, an incorrect planning parameter, or a delayed supplier response could quickly become a real operational problem. That experience is still the reason I pay attention to what happens after a purchasing decision is made.

Over the years, I have worked across manufacturing, construction, and enterprise procurement. I have sourced suppliers, managed purchase orders, followed materials into production, resolved invoice and receiving issues, reviewed contracts, and built reports when the existing data was not giving people a clear answer.

I am naturally drawn to work that needs some untangling. I like sitting with the people involved, understanding where the process breaks, and following the issue from the original request to the final outcome. Sometimes the answer is a better supplier decision. Sometimes it is a clearer workflow, a system change, or simply giving the right person visibility at the right time.
